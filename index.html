fYT Rating

Check and control your YouTube video ratings using your own Google account.

About fYT Rating

fYT Rating shows whether you have liked a YouTube video and lets you like or unlike it, using your own Google account.

It can also answer rating requests from other apps on your device that you explicitly allow, so a launcher or widget can display and control your rating without handling Google sign-in itself.

How it works

fYT Rating uses your Google account to access your YouTube rating information. Authentication is performed by Google. The application does not ask you to provide your Google password to fYT Rating.

Requests from other apps are supported only when you explicitly allow those apps to use the functionality.
